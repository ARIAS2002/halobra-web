Account Deletion

Last updated: August 13, 2026

How to delete your account

You can delete your Halobra account directly from within the app. This action is permanent and cannot be undone. Follow these steps:

  1. Open Halobra and sign in.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. In the ACCOUNT section, tap "Delete my account."
  4. Enter your password to confirm.
  5. Tap "Delete my account forever."

What data is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes, in cascade:

  • Your account and sign-in credentials.
  • Your business profile (name, phone number, business name, and license number).
  • All saved clients.
  • All estimates and their PDFs.
  • Your custom pricing catalog.

What data is retained

No personal data is retained after your account is deleted. Voice recordings and photos are never stored in the first place — they are processed only in memory to generate your estimates — so there is nothing to delete there.

Subscription transaction records are retained by the app stores (Google Play and Apple), not by Halobra.

Need help?

If you are unable to access your account to delete it yourself, email us at admin@halobra.com and we will process your request.
